Frequently Asked Questions

What accounting services does Excel Expedition provide?

We provide bookkeeping, invoicing, accounts receivable, accounts payable, account reconciliation, bookkeeping cleanup, payroll, month-end closing, financial reporting, sales tax, compliance, data cleanup, accounting migrations, and other accounting support services.

Do you specialize in U.S. accounting?

Yes. Our expertise is focused on serving U.S. businesses, with 10+ years of experience working in the U.S. accounting environment.

Can you handle sales tax in multiple states?

Yes. We have experience with sales tax matters across 40+ states, including registrations, filings, nexus determination, notices, and ongoing compliance.

What is sales tax nexus?

Sales tax nexus determines whether a business has sufficient connection with a state to create a sales tax collection obligation. We can help businesses assess their nexus requirements and manage related compliance matters.

Can you help if my books are a mess?

Yes. Bookkeeping cleanup and data cleanup are among our areas of expertise. We can review your records, identify discrepancies, correct issues, and organize your books.

Can you migrate my accounting data?

Yes. We have experience with accounting data migrations and can help businesses move and organize financial information when changing accounting systems.

Do you provide payroll services?

Yes. Our payroll support includes recurring pay runs, payroll setup, statutory deductions, and payroll-related compliance requirements.

Can you customize my Chart of Accounts?

Yes. We can review your current Chart of Accounts and customize it based on your business structure and reporting requirements.

What accounting software do you work with?

We have experience with platforms including QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, Bill.com, AppFolio, and other business and productivity platforms.

Can you integrate accounting software with my e-commerce platform?

Yes. We have experience with integrations such as QuickBooks Online with Amazon and BigCommerce, as well as Shopify, Stripe, and other platforms with accounting systems such as Xero.

Do you provide management reports?

Yes. We can prepare customized monthly and management reports designed to provide useful insight into your business performance.
